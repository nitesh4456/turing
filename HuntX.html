About Hunt-X

This treasure hunt features teams of two competing against 4-5 other teams. Participants solve riddles and clues at college landmarks, testing their teamwork and strategy. The first team to solve all clues and reach the final destination wins.

Rules & Guidelines

  • Each team must consist of 2 players.
  • Only 4-5 teams can participate in one round.
  • Teams must stay together at all times.
  • No use of mobile phones or external help is allowed.
  • Clues must not be removed or damaged.
  • Fair play is mandatory.

Format & Winning Criteria

Format

  • The event will start with a brief introduction and rule explanation.
  • Each team will receive the first clue at the starting point.
  • Solving each clue leads teams to the next location where another clue is hidden.
  • Teams must follow the sequence of clues until the final destination.
  • The team that completes the hunt first with all correct clues wins.

Device & Connectivity

  • Mobile phones are not allowed during the hunt.
  • No internet or external communication devices are permitted.
  • Teams must rely only on their problem-solving and observation skills.

Scoring System

  • 1st Team to finish – Winner
  • 2nd Team to finish – Runner-up
  • If two teams finish at nearly the same time, accuracy of clues and rule compliance will be considered.
  • Any rule violation may lead to a penalty or disqualification.

Winning Criteria

  • The first team to solve all clues and reach the final target wins. The second team to reach will be declared runner-up.

Disqualification Criteria

  • Use of unfair means or external assistance.
  • Tampering with clues or misleading other teams intentionally.
  • Team splitting up during the hunt.

Note

  • Participants must report at the starting point 15 minutes before the event begins.
  • Organizers’ decisions during the hunt will be final and binding.
