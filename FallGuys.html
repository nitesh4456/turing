FALL GUYS

About Fall Guys

Fall Guys Tournament with around 50 players for a fun and competitive gaming event. Players will compete in different rounds like obstacle courses, survival, and team games in custom lobbies. Points will be given based on performance in each round. The top players will reach the finals, and one ultimate winner will be crowned. In case of a tie, tie-breaker rules will decide the winner.

Rules & Guidelines

  • The tournament will be played using laptops only.
  • This tournament is strictly conducted in Solo Mode.
  • The tournament is open to all registered college students.
  • Approximately 50 players will participate.
  • Each participant must complete registration before the event starts.
  • Players must join the lobby on time, late entries may not be allowed.
  • No match restarts will be provided due to personal connectivity issues.
  • Players are responsible for their own internet connection and device.
  • Bring your own devices for the game

Format & Winning Criteria

Format

  • There will be 5 matches

Device & Connectivity

  • Players will participate using laptops only.
  • All players must ensure a stable internet connection during the tournament.
  • Any connection issues during the match will be the player’s responsibility.

Scoring System

  • The winner of the final round receives 10 points.
  • All other finalists receive 5 points each.
  • Players eliminated before the final round receive no points.

Winning Criteria

  • After all scheduled matches are completed, the player with the highest total points will be declared the tournament winner.

Disqualification Criteria

  • Refusal to follow tournament schedule or official instructions.
  • Intentional teaming or helping another player in Solo Mode is strictly prohibited.
  • Participants will be disqualified if they are found:
    • Using hacks, cheats, scripts, or third-party software.
    • Modifying game files or system settings to manipulate gameplay.
